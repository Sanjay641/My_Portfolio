About me

I am an electrical engineer based in Milan, specialising in renewable energy, energy storage and electric mobility. I hold a BE in Electrical and Electronics Engineering (CGPA 8.04/10) and am currently in the 3rd semester of my MSc at Politecnico di Milano (degree in progress, expected September 2027).
